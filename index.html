How it works

Naksha se chaabi tak - six clear steps.

नक्शे से चाबी तक

No confusion, no surprises. You always know what stage your house is at, what it costs, and what happens next.

  1. 1

    मुलाक़ात

    Free site visit

    We visit your plot, understand your family's needs and your budget. No charges, no commitment.

  2. 2

    नक्शा

    Naksha & 3D design

    Floor plan as per Vastu and bylaws, plus a 3D front elevation so you see the house before it is built.

  3. 3

    नक्शा पास

    Approvals handled

    We prepare and follow up the approval file with the authority so you don't have to run around offices.

  4. 4

    अनुमान

    Written estimate

    Item-wise estimate (BOQ) and a simple agreement - materials, brands, timeline and payment stages in writing.

  5. 5

    निर्माण

    Construction + updates

    Foundation to finishing under one supervisor. Photos and progress on WhatsApp every week.

  6. 6

    गृह प्रवेश

    Handover & griha pravesh

    Final walkthrough, snag fixing and keys in your hand - with our support even after you move in.

Questions

Things people ask us before they start.

अक्सर पूछे जाने वाले सवाल

Ask your own question
Do you only build, or can you also help me buy a plot?

Both. Many families come to us first for a plot and then we build their house on it. We also sell ready-made houses, so you can choose whichever suits your budget and timeline.

How much does it cost to build a house in Haldwani?

It depends on area, number of floors and the finish you choose. Use our cost calculator for a quick idea, then call us - after a free site visit we give a written, item-wise estimate.

Will you get my naksha (map) passed?

Yes. We prepare the drawings, submit the approval file and follow it up with the authority. Government fees are charged at actuals, with receipts.

Can I give only the labour contract and buy material myself?

Yes, we take both turnkey (material + labour) and labour-only contracts. We will guide you on quantity and brands either way.

How will I know what is happening at site?

You get regular photos and updates on WhatsApp, and you are welcome to visit the site any time. Payments are linked to completed stages.

Do you help with home loans?

We help you prepare the documents banks ask for - approved map, estimate and stage certificates - so the loan process goes smoothly.
